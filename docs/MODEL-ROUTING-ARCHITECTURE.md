## Implemented flow

The public multilingual route checks input size and explicit contact-number/email privacy before model routing. It does not classify debt, illness, names, religious scope, personal rulings or instruction attempts through subject-word regular expressions. Legacy unadmitted library entry points retain their earlier screening; they do not override the new server-only admitted route. Client requests cannot supply admission, a routing gloss or search terms.

Luna routes the unchanged original input. Uncertain or non-textual classifications receive one independent Terra route, with no first label placed in its prompt. Failed review stops the request; provider errors never manufacture acceptance. Both attempts and their usage remain in the sealed record, and usage accounting deduplicates actual reservation IDs. Language confidence is separate from scope confidence. A genuinely unclear question receives a short English/Arabic clarification instead of being mislabeled as unknown language. Confirmed personal rulings, sensitive requests, instruction overrides and unrelated tasks remain referred.

An authenticated whole Arabic source quotation retains its existing detection shortcut. Its source match admits textual search; it never automatically proves an attached interpretation. Source-ID, original quotation, source hash, budget, size and request controls continue to apply.

Every decisive source review now uses Terra under the Luna configuration and receives both the English and Arabic draft explanations. Approval requires a separate explanation-preservation flag as well as the original meaning and source-relationship checks. Wrong conditions, unsupported reasons, invented quotations, wrong references or independent Hadith-authentication claims must be rejected. A rejected explanation gets at most one stronger reassessment on the same evidence packet and another source review. The first failed review and every additional cost are retained; a prose failure does not trigger a new evidence search. Missing explanation approval fails closed.

Proof may use a primary source unit plus up to seven additional necessary units. Each selected ID resolves to its complete immutable source bytes; units are separately attributed rather than concatenated into an invented quotation. Unknown/duplicated IDs, uncited units, changed bytes and shortened proving text are refused. The evidence packet remains bounded to eight primary cards and forty supplied primary/context units.

Qualified explanations now permit multi-part questions when supported portions and unresolved portions are explicit and the complete explanation independently passes source review. They do not upgrade an insufficient broad verdict. During local or online recovery, up to four earlier directly relevant support/partial cards are reauthenticated and retained alongside replacement search results, within the eight-card limit. This improves continuity but does not guarantee complete retrieval or preservation of every candidate.
