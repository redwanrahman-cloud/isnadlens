## Review standard

A response is satisfactory only if its conclusion answers the original question, the relationship is grounded in genuine admitted evidence, all material clauses and relevant qualifications are preserved, and the visible explanation does not introduce a misleading religious instruction. The decision badge alone does not establish success. Mechanical checks compare original inputs, exact admitted source bytes and nested record seals. Developer review examines the selected proving unit, model propositions and explanation against the locked source key; an equally valid alternative admitted source can support the conclusion. Withholding a plainly answerable in-scope question is safe but is not counted as a satisfactory answer.

These checks are developer source-grounded review, not independent scholarly certification. The 33 SAR development cap and separate 15 SAR judging allocation remain in place. The runner stops on provider/budget failures rather than making an incomplete run appear to pass.
