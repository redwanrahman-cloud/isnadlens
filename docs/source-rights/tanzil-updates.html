Text Updates

This page keeps track of changes made to the Tanzil quran texts. You can join our mailing list to get automatically informed of the updates to the texts.

The latest release of our quran texts can be obtained from the download page.

Version 1.1

  • Release date: February 12, 2021

Text Updates

  • All Texts:
    • Three occurrences of بَعْدَمَا in ayas 2:181, 8:6, and 13:37 are replaced by بَعْدَ مَا.
  • Uthmani:
    • Two occurrences of يَـٰصَىٰحِبَىِ are replaced by يَـٰصَـٰحِبَىِ in ayas 12:39 and 12:41 to match Medina Mushaf.
    • HIGH_NOON (U+06E8) is replaced by TATWEEL + HIGH_NOON in the word نُـۨجِى in aya 21:88.
    • SMALL_WAW (U+06E5) is replaced by TATWEEL + SMALL_WAW in the word لِيَسُـۥـُٔوا in aya 17:7.
    • SMALL_YEH (U+06E6) in the middle of words are replaced by TATWEEL + HIGH_YEH (U+06E7), e.g. in إِبْرَ‌ٰهِـۧمَ.
    • All occurrences of لءا are replaced by لـَٔا, e.g. in بِالْـَٔاخِرَةِ.
  • Uthmani Minimal:
    • Capital Yeh's before Hamza (e.g. in شَيء) are replaced by dotless Yeh (ى) to remain compatible with Medina Mushaf.
  • Simple:
    • The following changes are made to increase compatibility with Uthmani text:
      • 39:56 يا حَسرَتا ⇒‎ يا حَسرَتىٰ
      • 5:31 يا وَيلَتا ‎⇒ يا وَيلَتىٰ
      • 17:32 الزِّنا ⇒‎ الزِّنىٰ

Version 1.0.2

  • Release date: May 12, 2008
  • Texts affected: Simple
  • Changes:
    • An extra sukun above noon at the beginning of sura Al-Ghalam (68:1) is removed in the simple text.

Version 1.0.1

  • Release date: February 4, 2008
  • Texts affected: Uthmani
  • Changes:
    • A shadda is added above beh (due to idgham rule) in the clause بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيم at the beginning of suras 95 and 97.
