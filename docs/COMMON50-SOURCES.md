# Fifty everyday questions: source review

The dataset `artifacts/common-question-baseline-50-2026-10-04.json` contains exactly 50 distinct natural questions. B01–B20 adapt the earlier reviewed cases into question wording; B21–B50 add 30 different propositions. Nine translation variants are excluded from this count. Topics are representative everyday subjects, not a measured ranking of popular searches.

Online primary research on 4 October 2026 used the [official HadeethEnc homepage](https://hadeethenc.com/en/home), which organizes worship/jurisprudence, creed, and manners and features foundational Hadiths, and [QuranEnc publication/API documentation](https://quranenc.com/en/home/api/). Official topic entry links include [jurisprudence](https://hadeethenc.com/en/browse/category/4) and [manners](https://hadeethenc.com/en/browse/category/5). Earlier direct category requests encountered rate/size limits. Neither topic counts nor API traffic establish search popularity.

Every new Quran case was reviewed against the immutable admitted Tanzil Arabic verse and admitted QuranEnc English Rowwad v1.0.19 reading aid. Dataset source witnesses record the exact display quotation hash; each case links the official Arabic location and corresponding published translation. The dataset contains original question phrasing, not copied translation paragraphs. The reading aid is not silently treated as a new Arabic evidence corpus.

New coverage includes ablution, prayer direction, Friday prayer, intoxicated prayer, fasting hours, secret charity, hurtful charity, kind speech, debt hardship, orphan property, impartial testimony, ridicule, home privacy, greetings, trusts, covenants, waste, mercy, religious compulsion, divine oneness, unsupported knowledge, spying, news verification, written debts, pork, carrion, seafood, pilgrimage ability, and patience with prayer. Sources are individual verses listed in the dataset; related text is not sufficient unless it supports the particular proposition.

Expectations remain provisional agent review, not a scholar-certified gold standard. B05 lacks direct admitted English smile-as-charity support; it is an evidence-coverage test, not a global denial. B15 tests potentially mistaken Quran attribution, whose sincerity/intentions overlap needs human review. B20 tests an overly broad necessity exception and requires review rather than a personal fatwa. B17 weather and B18 personal illness/fasting should be refused before paid evaluation. B14 invented app and B16 partly unsupported compound proposition remain insufficient. B19 deliberately asks a proposition contradicted by the reviewed source; question punctuation must not convert it into a supported answer.

New questions retain important conditions: hardship for debt relief, ability for pilgrimage, fixed term for recording debts, ordinary conditions for prohibited foods, and evildoer attribution for news verification. B38/B41 mercy questions do not establish unconditional individual salvation. B48 seafood tests the stated general Quran provision without settling school-specific animal classifications. No case authorizes adjudicating an individual medical, financial, or family dispute.

For live evaluation submit only `claim`, `input_language`, and `corpus_selection`; never supply reviewer locators, witness hashes, explanations, or expected labels as search hints. Report actual results separately from expectations. Measure retrieval recall, quotation integrity, semantic coverage, refusal behavior, and audit provenance independently; a correctly hashed unrelated verse does not pass the semantic check. The dataset preparation made no paid model calls and changed no application implementation or admitted source text.

## Independent reference-answer audit

Each question now carries `reference_answer`, `reference_status`, `reference_links`, source quotation hashes, and `reference_accuracy_eligible`. Initial expected verdicts remain unchanged and are explicitly preserved as history. The reference is independent source reading, not the application's generated answer and not scholar certification. Forty-four questions have supported or contradicted primary-text references; six (B14–B18 and B20) remain unresolved or outside scope for factual-accuracy scoring.

B05 required a material correction to the evaluation interpretation. The [published Arabic smile-as-charity report](https://sunnah.global/hadeeth/ar/show/66237) identifies the report as Hasan and cites al-Tirmidhi 1956. The [publisher record](https://hadeethenc.com/ar/browse/hadith/66237) is already in the admitted Arabic corpus, with its unchanged publisher grade. Its English edition is absent. The initial insufficient expectation therefore represented English retrieval coverage, not uncertainty about the established report. The [Dorar record and source attribution](https://dorar.net/h/sSEjvf0G?osoul=1) provide an additional publication reference; the bibliographic report was checked without adding any new corpus or reproducing publisher paragraphs.

Score resolved factual accuracy only on the 44 eligible references. Report answer coverage and incorrect confident answers alongside it. An insufficient result for an established answer is a safe abstention but an unresolved coverage failure, never a resolved factual success merely because it agrees with an earlier expected abstention. For B14/B15/B16/B20 evaluate calibration and evidence boundaries separately; weather and individual health questions test scope refusal. The qualified reference exceptions and personal-ruling exclusions remain material.
