Developer review is not independent linguistic or scholarly certification. Categories are inspired by published question collections, not frequency analytics. The 35 yes / 15 no split has a 70% always-yes baseline.

Some primary verses recur because they contain different propositions: head wiping versus earlier face/arm washing; work after Friday prayer versus leaving trade before it; marital intimacy versus earlier fasting times; adornment versus avoiding waste. These are distinct facets, not paraphrases of previous questions.

The 15 negative cases contain an explicit incompatible proposition; absence alone is never the expected basis for contradiction. Hajj/tamattu qualifications and pregnant-divorce context are stated. No personal case or new fatwa is scored as a settled textual answer.
