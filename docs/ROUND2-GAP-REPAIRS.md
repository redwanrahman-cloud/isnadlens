## Meaning of the baseline

27/30 is **90% satisfactory responses**; 26/30 is **86.7%**. Our denominator includes three correctly referred boundary requests, so this metric should not be advertised as overall religious accuracy. Incorrect decisive answers, withholding, religious-answer coverage, citation integrity and language presentation are separate measurements. A 90% useful-response baseline is a practical minimum for the competition prototype, while a wrong confident religious conclusion remains a distinct failed requirement. Thirty curated questions do not establish a population-wide reliability guarantee or independent religious certification. Statistical guidance likewise treats uncertainty in proportions explicitly, especially with small samples: [NIST confidence-interval guidance](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm).
